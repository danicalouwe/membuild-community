Our house rules

Six short rules that keep this a place people want to come back to.

1

Be kind

Assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, never with people.

2

Share what worked

Share what worked for you, and what didn’t. It helps everyone learn.

3

No pitching in comments

Promote your own work in your intro, not under other people’s posts.

4

Keep it on topic

{{topic}}, and the people learning it. Pick the right topic when you post.

5

Protect privacy

What’s shared here stays here. No screenshots of members without asking.

6

Report, don’t argue

See something off? Tap ⋯ › Report. A moderator will look at it.

What happens if a rule is broken

1

A friendly note

A moderator messages you first.

2

A short pause

Posting is paused for a few days if it happens again.

3

Removed

For harm, hate or spam, straight away.
