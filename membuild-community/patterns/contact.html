Where are my courses?

In the Classroom, inside the community. Your progress is saved there.

Can I go at my own pace?

Account and login

I forgot my password

On the login page, tap “Forgot password?” and we’ll email you a link.

How do I change my email?

Open your profile in the community and tap Edit.

Billing

How do I cancel?

Open Billing in your menu and tap Cancel. It takes two clicks.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Write to us within 14 days of your first payment and we’ll refund it, no questions. How refunds work →

Where are my receipts?

In Billing, in your menu. Each payment has its receipt.

Using it

How do I post?

Tap the box at the top of Home, write a line, and tap Post.

How do I get the app on my phone?

Open the community on your phone and tap “Add to your phone” in the menu.
