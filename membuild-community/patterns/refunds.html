In short

Cancel any time in two clicks. You keep access until the end of what you paid for.

1. Cancelling

Open Billing in your menu and tap Cancel.

Your plan stops renewing. You keep everything until the end of the period you paid for.

2. Refunds

Changed your mind soon after paying?

Write to us within 14 days of your first payment and we’ll refund it. After that, cancelling stops the next payment.

3. Failed payments

If a payment doesn’t go through, we’ll email you.

You can update your card from Billing. We try again a few times before pausing access.
