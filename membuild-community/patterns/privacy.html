In short

We collect only what the community needs, never sell it, and you can download or delete it any time.

1. What we collect

Your name, email and what you post here.

If you pay, our payment provider handles your card; we never see or store the number. We also keep basic logs, like when you last signed in, to keep accounts safe.

2. How we use it

To run the community.

We show your posts to members, send the emails you chose in “Emails you get”, and use what you share only to make the community work better for you.

3. Who sees it

Members see your profile and posts.

Moderators can see reports and messages that are reported to them. We never sell your information, and we only share it when the law requires it.

4. Your choices

You’re in charge of your information.

Download a copy, or delete your account and everything in it, any time from Privacy in your menu. Turn emails up, down or off from “Emails you get”.

5. Questions about your privacy?

Email {{email}} and we’ll reply within a few days.
